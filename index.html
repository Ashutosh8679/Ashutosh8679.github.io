About Me

I'm a technically driven engineering student from Pune with a deep focus on robotics, embedded systems, and system-level integration. I'm most interested in projects where hardware and software have to work together tightly — sensors, actuators, control loops, and real-time communication.

My work spans dual-axis solar tracking gimbals, gesture-controlled surveillance robots, soft robotic grippers, and low-level embedded workstations. I'm drawn to problems that require understanding the entire stack — from circuit-level design to multithreaded software architecture.

Outside of project work I run Linux daily (i3 + zsh), enjoy building from schematics, and am progressively working toward ROS2-based robotic arms and edge AI applications.

"Engineering is most interesting when the system you're building has to deal with the physical world — uncertainty, noise, and all."
